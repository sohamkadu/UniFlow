Frequently Asked Questions

UniFlow is a unified stablecoin interface powered by Circle's App Kit. It aggregates your USDC balances across multiple networks (like Base and Arbitrum Sepolia) into a single, cohesive balance that can be managed and spent instantly on the Arc network.

When you deposit USDC from any supported source chain, UniFlow switches your wallet to that chain and routes the funds using Circle's cross-chain gateway. The system processes the transfer and updates your unified balance automatically.

No, UniFlow does not charge any platform fees. You only pay standard gas fees required by the respective blockchain networks to approve, deposit, or spend your stablecoins.

Your unified balance is spendable anywhere on the Arc Testnet. When you execute a spend transaction, Circle's gateway settles the funds directly to the recipient address on Arc instantly.

Yes. UniFlow is a fully client-side interface that connects directly to your Web3 wallet (e.g. MetaMask) and Circle's SDK. All transactions are securely signed in your wallet, and your private keys are never shared or stored on any server.
